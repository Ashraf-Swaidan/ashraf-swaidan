# Image prompt: spreadsheet chaos → one system (for image AI)

Use this as the **master prompt** for Midjourney, DALL·E, Ideogram, etc. Replace the bracketed notes with your real export settings if the tool requires aspect ratio or style tokens separately.

---

## Prompt (copy-paste)

```
Editorial illustration, clean modern business aesthetic, soft studio lighting, slight isometric angle. Left side: chaotic operations “spreadsheet hell” — overlapping semi-transparent worksheet grids, endless rows of tiny cells, tangled arrows, sticky notes, fragmented labels suggesting inventories, branches, sales, wallets, suppliers, expenses, roles, and conflicting formulas; muted greens and grays with subtle red error markers, overwhelming but not gory, no readable real company data, no logos, no Microsoft branding.

Center: a bold, minimal curved arrow or light trail flowing left-to-right, suggesting relief and transition.

Right side: a sleek floor-standing retail / trade-show display kiosk with a thin bezel monitor (generic futuristic hardware, no Apple logo). On the screen, COMPOSITE PLACEHOLDER: a crisp inset rectangle reserved for a UI screenshot — leave a clearly empty framed area with subtle “screen glare” so I can paste a real product screenshot later; the frame should read as a live analytics insights dashboard (soft charts, calm density), but do not invent fake UI text — use subtle abstract chart shapes and blurred blocks only.

Mood: calm, trustworthy, “we outgrew the spreadsheet,” premium product marketing, high detail, 8k, shallow depth of field, neutral background gradient (warm off-white to cool gray), no people, no hands, no text in the image except meaningless abstract glyphs on spreadsheet headers if any.
```

---

## Optional follow-up for your tool (paste screenshot)

After you generate the base image, many workflows allow **image-to-image**:

1. Prepare your sexiest **Papion insights** screenshot (crop to roughly **16:10** or match the screen inset in the mockup).
2. Inpaint or composite: replace only the **placeholder screen area** with your screenshot so edges, bezel, and lighting stay consistent.
3. If the model supports it, add a short instruction: *“Replace only the monitor screen content with the second image; keep bezel and stand unchanged.”*

---

## Negative / avoid

- No Microsoft Excel® logo, no copyrighted app icons, no celebrity, no gore.
- No legible confidential business names or phone numbers in sheet cells.
- Avoid overcrowding the kiosk side — the **right** should feel spacious and “one system,” the **left** should feel noisy.
