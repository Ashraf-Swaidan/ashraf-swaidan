## 1) Project identity

- **Project name:** SMARTAR
- **Type:** Shopify e-commerce store + custom theme
- **Domain:** Branded online retail (exact vertical TBD with owner)
- **Primary goal:** Sell through a distinctive, highly customizable storefront while using AI to improve discovery, guidance, or operations — without feeling like a generic theme install.

---

## 2) Problem this store solves

Off-the-shelf Shopify themes trade speed for sameness. Merchants who care about brand and conversion often fight:

- rigid section layouts and weak merchandising control,
- disconnected “AI apps” that feel bolted on,
- a storefront that looks like every other shop in the category.

SMARTAR addresses that with a **custom theme** built for flexibility and an **integrated AI layer** that belongs to the shopping experience.

---

## 3) Core capabilities (business language)

- **Custom Shopify theme** — section-driven homepage, product storytelling, and brand-specific UX (not a purchased template with logo swap).
- **Deep customization** — layout, typography, and merchandising blocks tuned to the brand without redeploying a separate codebase.
- **AI-integrated commerce** — intelligent assistance embedded in the store journey (specific flows: document as captures arrive).
- **Shopify ecosystem** — checkout, inventory, and ops remain on Shopify; the win is experience + theme craft + AI on that foundation.

---

## 4) Technical highlights (engineering angle)

- **Theme as product** — Liquid, JSON templates, section schema, and reusable blocks for merchant-friendly editing.
- **Performance-aware storefront** — video loops, image discipline, and mobile-first layouts suitable for conversion.
- **AI integration** — connect assistive features to catalog/context (implementation details: add when documented in repo or store admin).
